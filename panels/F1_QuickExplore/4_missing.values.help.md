<h4>Missing values</h4>
A plot of all missing values is produced. The bars at the top show how much is
missing (grey) and what is not (red). Below the bars all possible combinations of
missing versus not missing are plotted in a table-like plot. Next to the plot are
the frequencies of the observed combinations.<br>
The blue button labeled "GET TABLE OF ROW COMBINATIONS" produces a table-like
representation of the plot. If this is very complex it can take a while to load.
